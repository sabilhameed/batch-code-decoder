How the code works

A batch code is the last digit of the production year followed by the day of the year, padded to three digits.

CodeReads asProduction date
4001Year …4, day 11 January 2024
5182Year …5, day 1821 July 2025
0366Year …0, day 36631 December 2020

Because only one digit records the year, a code repeats every ten years. This tool picks the most recent year that matches the digit and is not in the future, and tells you when an older reading is also possible.

Expiry follows the same rule as the master file: three years from production for skincare and makeup, four years for fragrance, keeping the same day and month.
